## Brand Story

### The Origin
Hark began with a simple motto: **"Flavour is King"**

Founders **Sam McKay** and **Theo Engela** had a clear vision: ignore the condescending vibe of specialty coffee culture and conventional thinking about roasting. The goal? Simply delicious coffee.

### The Innovation: Air Roasting
After years of pursuit, Hark developed a **radical approach** to roasting: **100% convection energy** (air roasting / fluid bed roasting).

**Why this matters:**
- Traditional drum roasting uses conduction (beans touching hot metal) — inconsistent
- Air roasting surrounds beans with perfectly temperature-controlled air — consistent
- Hark's roaster micro-adjusts temperature **10 times per second**
- Eliminates surface burns and the unpleasant flavours they cause
- When they perfect a roasting curve, they can **repeat it exactly** every time

### The Claim
> "Frankly (and this is a big claim, we know!) it can now do things that simply are not possible to achieve with drum roasting."

Hark believes they have **Australia's most advanced coffee roaster and software**.

> "Yes! We have 100% plant-based, compostable Nespresso-compatible pods. We offer both our Humdinger blend and Swiss Water Decaf. Would you like a sample pack?"

### "What makes your coffee different?"
> "We're one of the few roasters in Australia using air roasting technology. Our custom-built roaster micro-adjusts temperature 10 times per second, giving us radical consistency that drum roasters simply can't match. Every bag tastes exactly as intended."

### "Do you deliver to offices?"
